Case study, NinjaToolKit v9.0.2, November 2026

A model reads the whole server.Code holds it to the evidence.An engineer decides every change.

NinjaToolKit is an audit and remediation platform for managed Windows Server estates and WatchGuard firewalls. I designed it, built it and released it: one self-contained program for a whole engineering team, its own agents on the servers, and a harness that takes one server from a first read to a fix that proves itself.

At the release: 139,481 lines of Python in eleven layers, 179 deterministic checks, 6,145 tests passing, 80,161 requests through a soak of two hours forty with no error, 318 of 318 audit fields kept by code's own check of what the model wrote, and every one of 1,923 value-carrying paths in 54 firewall exports accounted for.

Twelve acts, about twenty-two minutes. Ten drawn scenes show how the harness works inside, every figure in them measured. Between them the real v9.0.1 program works five invented clients, thirty-one servers and seven firewalls, recorded as it happened or replayed from its own record. Every word the model says is what it wrote, and every dollar it spent is in its ledger.
